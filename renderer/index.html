⌾

Protected information

In “Just me,” questions about identifiers and immigration facts route automatically to the encrypted vault. Those facts are never embedded, trained on, or sent to web search, and identifiers remain redacted unless you explicitly request the full value.

＋

Plus menu

The composer’s plus button contains Remember, Train, Think, Add image, and Add file. Files enter the managed index; images are read locally and their text is attached and indexed. Protected-looking content is rejected from ordinary memory and training.

◎

Automatic routing

The app chooses the local model, your laptop knowledge, the live web, or both. Personal questions stay local; current information and version-sensitive coding use web evidence; ordinary coding runs directly on Gemma. Protected questions always remain in the encrypted vault.

●

Local voice dictation

Tap the microphone, speak naturally in English or Telugu, then tap the stop square. Stop immediately turns off the microphone and counter before local MLX Whisper transcription begins. Temporary audio is deleted afterward.

⌥

Jarvis Quick Chat

Tap Option-Space to open or hide chat. Hold Option-Space to listen, speak naturally, then release to transcribe, answer from your personal context, and speak the response.

◇

Three-layer intelligence

Gemma supplies general reasoning and language ability, your LoRA adapter shapes communication style, and local plus live retrieval supplies personal and current factual knowledge. Sources remain separate so they can update without retraining the model.

◌

Private chat

Messages in a private chat are kept only in memory for the current request and are not written to conversation history.

↥

Backup and migration

Create an encrypted portable backup before changing Macs or uploading to private cloud storage. Model downloads and development caches are reinstalled separately.
